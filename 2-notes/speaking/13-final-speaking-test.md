# Final Speaking Test Bank - Bộ Câu Hỏi Cuối Khóa

Bộ này tổng hợp từ question bank giáo viên gửi, audio phản xạ, và các sheet speaking theo chủ đề. Khi ôn, không cần học thuộc một bài dài; hãy học **answer frame** rồi thay thông tin cá nhân của mình vào.

## 01. Cách trả lời an toàn

Mẫu ngắn đủ dùng cho Pre-IELTS:

1. Trả lời trực tiếp.
2. Thêm 1 lý do bằng `because`.
3. Thêm 1 ví dụ/cụ thể hóa nếu có thể.

Ví dụ:

| Question | Safe answer frame |
|---|---|
| Do you like sports? | Yes, I do. I like badminton because it helps me keep fit. I usually play it twice a week. |
| What is your dream job? | My dream is to become a teacher because I love working with kids. I think it is a meaningful job. |
| Tell me about one trip you remember the most. | The trip I remember the most was my trip to Da Nang. I went there with my family last summer. We enjoyed the beach and the local food. |

## 05. Topic frames cần học thuộc mềm

### Work / Study

- `I want to be/become a/an...`
- `My dream is to become a/an...`
- `It is a good job for me because...`
- `I love working with...`
- `It is hard work, but the pay is good.`

### Appearance

- `He/She is in his/her twenties/thirties/forties.`
- `He/She is of average height.`
- `He/She is quite slim/well-built.`
- `He/She has long straight black hair.`
- `He/She often wears casual clothes.`

### Personality

- `He/She is really neat and always keeps things tidy.`
- `He/She is great to be with because he/she makes me laugh.`
- `He/She is friendly and easy to talk to.`
- `He/She is kind and generous.`
- `He/She is kind of shy and not very talkative.`

### Weekend / Hobbies

- `I often go out for dinner / go to the cinema / go for coffee / go shopping.`
- `Watching films is a good way to relax.`
- `I am really into reading books about...`
- `I am keen on listening to music.`
- `I am a big fan of...`

### Trip

- `The trip I remember the most was my trip to...`
- `I went there ... ago with my family/friends.`
- `We went sightseeing and enjoyed the local food.`
- `It was great/wonderful/fantastic/so-so/terrible.`
- `If I had a chance, I would love to visit this place again.`

### Restaurant / Food

- `We went to a seafood/steak/fast food restaurant.`
- `To start the meal, we had...`
- `The main dish was...`
- `For dessert, we ordered...`
- `It was tasty/delicious/fresh, but a little salty/oily.`

### Hometown

- `I come from... / I was born and raised in...`
- `It is in the north/south/centre of Vietnam.`
- `What I like about my hometown is that...`
- `The trouble is that...`
- `There are a few lakes/rivers/hills where I live.`

### Health

- `Yes, I have a healthy lifestyle because I eat a lot of fruit and vegetables.`
- `I do exercise every day.`
- `I drink enough water.`
- `If I have a headache, I often rest or take aspirin.`
- `If I have got a cold, I often go to bed with a hot drink.`
