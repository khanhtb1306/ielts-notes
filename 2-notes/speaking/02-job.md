# Job - Công Việc / Nghề Nghiệp Mơ Ước

## 01. What is your dream job? Why?

[[audio:12]]

**Mẫu câu:**

- I want to be/become a/an... / My dream is to become a/an...

**Why?**

- It is a good job for me because...
- I like helping people. / I love working with kids.
- It is hard work, but the pay is good. / It is a well-paid job.
- I get to meet a lot of people. / I have a chance to visit lots of countries.

## 02. What do you do? / What is your job?

[[audio:10]]

**Are you a student or do you work?**

[[audio:11]]

**Mẫu câu:**

- I'm a/an ... at ...
- I'm working as a/an ... at ...
- I'm a first-year student at ... university.

## 03. What do you like about your job?

- I really like... / I like ... a lot.
- I'm well-paid. / I love working with kids.
- It is the people I work with. They are so nice.
- I get to meet so many people.

## 04. What do you dislike about your job?

- The only trouble is...
- I do the same thing every day. I'm really sick of it.
- I don't like the long hours. / I'm on my feet all day.
- The pay is not good. / I'm not well-paid.

## 05. Cách Khác Diễn Tả Thích / Không Thích

| Thích | Không thích |
|---|---|
| be into something | can't stand something |
| have a passion for something | hate something |
| be interested in something | not like something |
| love working with... | be sick of something |

## 06. Câu Hỏi Tương Tự

- Do you want to become a/an ... in the future? Why?
- What does your father/mother/brother/sister do?

## 07. Bài Mẫu Ngắn
