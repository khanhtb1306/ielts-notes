Điểm bắt đầu của lộ trình: giới thiệu tên, tuổi, quê, việc học/làm và sở thích.

## 02. Khung Trả Lời

- My name is...
- I am ... years old.
- I am from...
- I am a student. / I'm a first-year student at ... university.
- In my free time, I like/enjoy...

Từ vựng bậc học: a freshman, a sophomore, a junior, a senior; I'm in grade 12 / in my final year.

## 03. Câu Hỏi Về Trường Lớp (Lesson 9)

- What is your favourite subject at school?
- Do you like your school? Why?
- What do you usually do with your friends?

Gợi ý: *My favourite subject is English because it is useful and interesting. I like my school because the teachers are friendly and helpful.*

## 04. Bài Mẫu Ngắn

**Version 1 — Cứng, không filler (Band 4-5):**

> My name is Khanh. I am a student and I am from Hanoi. In my free time, I enjoy listening to music and watching films because they help me relax.

**Version 2 — Có filler + connector tự nhiên (Band 5-6):**

> **Well**, my name is Khanh. I'm a student, **and** I'm from Hanoi. **In my free time**, I really enjoy listening to music and watching films — **you know**, they help me relax after a long day.
