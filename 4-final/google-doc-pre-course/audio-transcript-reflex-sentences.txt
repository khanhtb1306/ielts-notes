SPEAKING PRE-IELTS

Topic
Questions
Name
1. What’s your name? 
2. How do you spell your name?
Age/ Birthday/Time
1. How old are you?
2. When is your birthday?
Hometown
1. Where do you come from?
2. What do you like about your hometown?
3. Is there anything you dislike about your hometown?
4. Are there any lakes or rivers/woods or forests/hills or mountains in your hometown? 
Jobs
School
1. What do you do?
2. Are you a student or are you working?
3. What is your dream job? Why?
Hobbies
1. What do you often do in your free time?
2. What do you often do on the weekend?
3. Do you like sports? How often?
4. Do you like watching movies?
What kinds of movies do you like?
What is your favorite movie?
5. Do you like reading books? 
6. Do you like listening to music? 
7. Do you like playing games? 
Daily routine/Time
1. What time is it in your country?
2. What time do you: 
- get up?
- go to school/work?
- have breakfast/lunch/dinner?
- come home from school/work?
- go to bed?
3. How do you go to school/work?
4. How often do you:
- phone your friend?
- clean the house?
- read the newspaper? 
- have friends round? (When you have friends round, what do you like to do?)
Family/
Describe people:
Appearance, Clothes
Personalities
1. Do you live in a big family?
2. What does your father/mother do?
3. What does he/she look like?
4. What kind of hair does he/she have?
5. What kinds of clothes does he/she often wear? (What kinds of clothes are you wearing?)
6. What is he like?
7. Is he friendly/ talkative/ humorous/ generous…?
8. What does he/she like?
House 
(Describe a room)
1. What is your favorite room in your house?
2. Can you describe your living room/ bedroom/ kitchen?
Entertainment
Vacations
1. Do you love traveling?
2. Tell me about one trip you remember the most.
3. Tell me about one trip you want to take in the future.
Food/drinks
1. What are your favorite foods/drinks? Are they good for you?
2. Do you like eating out? How often do you go to a restaurant?
Health and illness
1. Do you have a healthy diet?
2. Do you exercise? What exercise do you do?
3. Do you often feel stressed?
4. What should you do if you: 
- have a headache/toothache?
- have got a cold?
