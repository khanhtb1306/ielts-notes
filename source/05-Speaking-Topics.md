# 05. Speaking Topics - Tổng Hợp Theo Lộ Trình Học

File này gộp nội dung Speaking cũ vào một file duy nhất, đánh số theo quá trình học để dễ ôn. Nội dung không bị rút gọn thành ý mới; các mẫu câu, câu hỏi, từ vựng và bài mẫu cũ được giữ lại, chỉ được chuẩn hóa bố cục.

## 01. Bản Thân - Self Introduction

Phần này được thêm làm điểm bắt đầu lộ trình vì slide có yêu cầu introduce yourself và describe family members.

### 01.1. Câu hỏi thường gặp

1. What is your name?
2. How old are you?
3. Where are you from?
4. What do you do?
5. What are your hobbies?

### 01.2. Khung trả lời

- My name is...
- I am ... years old.
- I am from...
- I am a student.
- In my free time, I like/enjoy...

### 01.3. Bài mẫu ngắn

My name is Khanh. I am a student and I am from Hanoi. In my free time, I enjoy listening to music and watching movies because they help me relax.

## 02. Công Việc / Nghề Nghiệp Mơ Ước - Job

### 02.1. What is your dream job?

#### Mẫu câu trả lời

- I want to be/become a/an...
- My dream is to become a/an...

#### Why?

- It is a good job for me because...
- I like acting in front of lots of people.
- I like helping people.
- I like travel and flying.
- I love working with kids/children.
- It is hard work, but the pay is good.
- It is a well-paid job.
- I get to meet a lot of people.
- I have a chance to visit lots of countries.

### 02.2. What do you do? / What is your job?

#### Mẫu câu trả lời

- I'm a/an ... at ...
- I'm working as a/an ... at ...

### 02.3. What do you like about your job?

#### Mẫu câu trả lời

- I like ... a lot.
- I really like...
- I'm well-paid.
- I love working with kids.
- I think it is the people I work with. They are so nice/kind.
- I think my boss is great to work for.
- I get to meet so many people.

### 02.4. What do you dislike about your job?

Câu hỏi tương tự:

- Is there anything you don't like about your job?

#### Mẫu câu trả lời

- The only trouble is...
- I do the same thing every day.
- I'm really sick of it.
- I don't like the long hours.
- It is hard work and pretty tiring.
- I'm on my feet all day.
- The pay is not good.
- I'm not well-paid.

### 02.5. Cách khác để diễn tả thích / không thích

| Thích | Không thích |
|---|---|
| be into something | can't stand something |
| have a passion for something | hate something |
| be interested in something | not like something |
| love working with... | be sick of something |

### 02.6. Câu hỏi tương tự

- Do you want to become a/an ... in the future? Why?
- What is your father's/mother's/brother's/sister's job?
- What does your father/mother/brother/sister do?

### 02.7. Bài mẫu ngắn

My dream is to become a teacher. It is a good job for me because I love working with kids. It is hard work, but I think it is meaningful.

## 03. Gia Đình - Family

### 03.1. Do you live in a big family?

#### Mẫu câu trả lời

- No, I'm an only child. It's just me and my parents.
- No, I live in a nuclear/small family with my parents and my younger sister.
- Well, I come from quite a big/large family. I've got two brothers.

### 03.2. How many members/people are there in your family?

#### Mẫu câu trả lời

- There are four members/people in my family: my parents, my younger brother and me.

### 03.3. Have you got / Do you have any brothers/sisters/siblings?

#### Mẫu câu trả lời

- I have one younger brother and two elder sisters.
- I've got one younger brother and two elder sisters.

### 03.4. What does he/she like? / What are your hobbies?

#### Mẫu câu trả lời

- He/She is very interested in computers. We spend all our free time in computer stores and on the Internet.
- My sister is more interested in the arts. She likes music and she loves to read.
- My dad likes outdoor things. He is really into sports and swimming, and he always tells me I should get more interested in sports.
- Both of us love to learn new things about cultures.
- I'm keen on movies and sports.

### 03.5. Cấu trúc nói về sở thích

| Mẫu câu | Theo sau bằng | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| like / love / enjoy | noun hoặc V-ing | I enjoy reading books. |
| be interested in | noun hoặc V-ing | She is interested in music. |
| be really into | noun hoặc V-ing | My dad is really into sports. |
| be keen on | noun hoặc V-ing | I'm keen on movies and sports. |

### 03.6. Mở rộng thêm lý do vì sao thích hoạt động đó

- because it is interesting.
- because it is relaxing.
- because it helps me relax.
- because it is good for health.

### 03.7. Từ vựng cần nhớ

| English | Vietnamese |
|---|---|
| only child | con một |
| nuclear family | gia đình hạt nhân |
| small family | gia đình nhỏ |
| big/large family | gia đình lớn |
| younger brother/sister | em trai/em gái |
| elder brother/sister | anh trai/chị gái |
| parents | bố mẹ |
| siblings | anh chị em ruột |
| outdoor things | hoạt động ngoài trời |
| culture | văn hóa |

## 04. Ngoại Hình - Appearance

### 04.1. Câu hỏi thường gặp

1. What does he/she look like?
2. What does your father/mother look like?
3. Can you describe your father/mother?
4. What kinds of clothes does he/she often wear?

### 04.2. Age - Tuổi

- He/She is almost 25, but he/she doesn't look that old.
- He/She is in his/her twenties/thirties/forties.
- He/She is young/old/middle-aged.

### 04.3. General look - Ngoại hình chung

- He/She is very + adjective.

Adjectives:

- handsome - đẹp trai
- pretty - xinh đẹp
- beautiful - xinh đẹp
- cute - đáng yêu
- good-looking - ưa nhìn
- average-looking - bình thường

### 04.4. Height, weight and build - Chiều cao, cân nặng và vóc người

- He/She is not very tall, about ... centimeters.
- He/She is quite short, about ... centimeters.
- He/She is of average height.
- He/She weighs ... kilos.
- He/She is quite slim/thin/overweight/chubby/well-built.

### 04.5. Face and head - Mặt và đầu

- He/She has got dark/fair skin.
- She has got long straight black hair.
- He/She has got brown/blue eyes.
- She has got glasses.
- He has got a beard/moustache.

Từ vựng tóc:

- long - dài
- short - ngắn
- shoulder-length - ngang vai
- straight - thẳng
- wavy - xoăn lượn sóng
- curly - xoăn xù
- black - đen
- brown - nâu
- blonde - vàng hoe

### 04.6. Clothes - Quần áo

#### What kinds of clothes does he/she often wear?

- He/She often wears...

Clothes:

- shirts - áo sơ mi
- T-shirts - áo phông
- a pair of jeans - quần bò
- casual clothes - quần áo thường ngày

### 04.7. Bài mẫu ngắn

My father is in his forties. He is not very tall, but he is well-built. He has got short black hair and brown eyes. He often wears shirts and a pair of jeans.

## 05. Tính Cách - Personality

### 05.1. What is he/she/your father/your mother like?

#### Công thức trả lời

`He/She is + adjective + explanation.`

Ví dụ:

- He is really neat. He always keeps things tidy.
- She is great to be with. She makes me laugh all the time.

### 05.2. Gợi ý tính cách và giải thích

| Tính cách | Giải thích mẫu |
|---|---|
| be really neat/organized | He always keeps things tidy. |
| be great to be with | She makes me laugh all the time. |
| be funny/easy-going | He always tells jokes / does crazy things. |
| be friendly and easy to talk to | She is easy to talk to. |
| be outgoing/sociable | He loves going out and meeting people. |
| be kind and generous | She loves giving things and does not expect anything in return. |
| be kind of shy and not very talkative | He does not talk much. |
| always look like he is in a bad mood | He often looks unhappy. |
| be pretty serious | It is difficult to get him to laugh. |

Ghi chú thêm từ source cũ:

- when someone tells a really funny joke, he doesn't seem to think it is funny.

### 05.3. Is he friendly/talkative/humorous/generous?

#### Mức độ tính từ

| Mức độ | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|
| Rất | He is really/so/very + adjective. |
| Khá | He is quite/pretty/kind of + adjective. |
| Phủ định | He is not + adjective + at all. |

### 05.4. Ví dụ mẫu

My father is really neat. He always keeps things tidy. He is also great to be with because he makes me laugh all the time.

## 06. Nhà Ở Và Phòng Yêu Thích - House / Room

### 06.1. Do you live in a big house?

#### Mẫu câu trả lời

- Yes, my house is quite big.
- Yes, I live in a big house.
- No, my house is quite small.
- No, I live in a small house.

### 06.2. How many rooms does your house have?

#### Mẫu câu trả lời

- It has five rooms: a living room, a kitchen, a bathroom and two bedrooms.
- It's got five rooms: a living room, a kitchen, a bathroom and two bedrooms.

### 06.3. What is your favorite room in your house?

#### Mẫu câu trả lời

- My favorite room in my house is the bedroom/living room/kitchen.

#### Bedroom

- because I feel free and comfortable in my own room.
- because I can do whatever I want like studying, listening to music, watching films.

#### Kitchen

- because I love cooking.
- because I want to cook for my family.

#### Living room

- because I can spend time with my family there.
- We can watch TV together and have a chit-chat.

### 06.4. Can you describe your living room/bedroom/kitchen?

#### Mẫu câu trả lời

- It is comfortable, but it is pretty small.
- It is really big.
- It is huge.
- My room is painted in blue/white/pink.
- There is a desk, a chair, a bed and a wardrobe.

### 06.5. Áp dụng giới từ miêu tả vị trí đồ vật trong phòng

- My bed is in the middle of the room.
- The table is next to the bed.
- There are some pictures on the wall.

### 06.6. Từ vựng cần nhớ

| English | Vietnamese |
|---|---|
| living room | phòng khách |
| kitchen | nhà bếp |
| bathroom | phòng tắm |
| bedroom | phòng ngủ |
| desk | bàn học/bàn làm việc |
| chair | ghế |
| bed | giường |
| wardrobe | tủ quần áo |
| comfortable | thoải mái |
| huge | rất lớn |
| in the middle of | ở giữa |
| next to | bên cạnh |
| on the wall | trên tường |

## 07. Cuối Tuần Và Thời Gian Rảnh - Weekend / Free Time

### 07.1. What do you often do on the weekend / in your free time?

#### Mẫu câu

- I often + Verb.
- I don't do anything special.
- I'm not free on the weekend.
- I usually go away for the weekend.

#### Gợi ý hoạt động

- pack a lunch, ride to a park and have a picnic
- have a party
- go to a disco
- come over to my friend's house for dinner and watch a movie together
- go out for dinner
- see a movie / go to the cinema
- go for coffee after class/work/school
- go downtown
- go shopping

### 07.2. Other words to describe likes and dislikes

| Thích | Không thích |
|---|---|
| enjoy V-ing | can't stand something |
| be into something | hate something |
| have a passion for something | phủ định của các cụm thích |
| be interested in something |  |
| be keen on something |  |
| be crazy about something |  |
| be a big fan of something |  |

### 07.3. Do you like sports? How often do you play sports?

#### Từ vựng / cụm từ

- work out - tập thể dục
- get lots of exercise - tập thể dục nhiều
- get some exercise - tập thể dục một chút
- take walks - đi bộ
- go jogging to warm up - chạy bộ để khởi động
- help me be/keep in great shape - giúp tôi giữ dáng
- get big muscles - tăng cơ
- be really good at something - rất giỏi một thứ gì đó

#### How often?

- every day - hằng ngày
- always - luôn luôn
- usually - thường xuyên
- often - thường thường
- sometimes - thỉnh thoảng
- once/twice/three times a day/week - một/hai/ba lần một ngày/tuần

#### Mẫu câu phủ định

- I don't like exercising at all, but I do watch a lot of baseball on TV.
- I don't like doing anything too energetic.
- I don't like to sweat.

### 07.4. Do you like watching movies?

- Yes, watching movies is a good way to relax/chill out.
- No, I don't like watching movies. I often spend my free time reading books/listening to music/hanging out with my friends/cooking.

### 07.5. What is your favorite movie?

- My favorite movie is...
- It is one of the funniest/most interesting/best movies I've seen.
- If you want a good laugh, you should see it.
- It is worth seeing.
- The story is good/interesting/fantastic.
- The special effects are great/terrific.
- The main character is pretty good/amazing/good-looking.
- The music/soundtrack is excellent/nice.

### 07.6. Do you like reading books? What do you like to read most?

- I'm interested in reading books about animals/nature/culture/languages.
- I'm really into reading books about animals/nature/culture/languages.

### 07.7. Do you like listening to music?

- I'm keen on listening to music.
- I'm fond of listening to music.
- I often listen to my favorite songs when I'm relaxing/taking a shower/doing the chores.

### 07.8. Who is your favorite singer?

- I'm a big fan of...
- He has a great/beautiful voice.
- He is also handsome/attractive.
- Có thể miêu tả thêm về ngoại hình và tính cách đã học.

### 07.9. Câu hỏi tương tự

- What does your father/mother/brother/sister often do in his/her free time?
- Does your father/mother/brother/sister often play sports/read books/watch movies/listen to music?

## 08. Du Lịch Và Chuyến Đi Đáng Nhớ - Trip / Travelling

### 08.1. Do you love travelling?

#### Câu trả lời đồng ý

Yes, definitely, because I can relax, spend time with my family and friends, learn new things, and gain new experiences.

#### Câu trả lời không đồng ý

- No, I don't, because I don't have enough time.
- No, I don't, because travelling is expensive.

#### Useful ideas

- relax
- spend time with my family
- spend time with my friends
- learn new things
- gain new experiences
- enjoy beautiful scenery
- try local food

### 08.2. Tell me about one trip you remember the most.

#### Bài mẫu từ source cũ

The trip I remember the most is my trip to Hue, Da Nang, and Hoi An.

I went there last year.

I went with my friends.

We went there by plane, and it took us about one hour to get there.

We went sightseeing, enjoyed the local food, visited many beautiful places, and took a lot of photos.

The trip was wonderful, and I had a great time.

If I have a chance, I would love to visit these places again in the future.

### 08.3. Useful Expressions

- The trip I remember the most is...
- I went there...
- I went with...
- We went there by...
- It took us about...
- We went sightseeing.
- We enjoyed the local food.
- We visited many beautiful places.
- We took a lot of photos.
- The trip was wonderful.
- I had a great time.
- If I have a chance, I would love to visit this place again in the future.

|---|---|

| trip | chuyến đi |
| sightseeing | tham quan |
| local food | món ăn địa phương |
| beautiful places | những nơi đẹp |
| plane | máy bay |
| friends | bạn bè |
| family | gia đình |
| experience | trải nghiệm |

| fantastic | rất tuyệt |

### 08.5. Useful Grammar

- Present Simple
- Simple Past
- because...
- and / also
- If I have a chance, I would love to...

## 09. Checklist Tự Ôn Speaking

1. Trả lời được mỗi chủ đề bằng ít nhất 2-3 câu.
2. Có ít nhất 1 lý do dùng `because`.
3. Có ít nhất 1 cụm từ vựng đúng chủ đề.
4. Dùng đúng thì: Present Simple cho thói quen, Past Simple cho trip, Future forms cho dự định.
5. Đọc to câu trả lời 3 lần và tự sửa phát âm.
