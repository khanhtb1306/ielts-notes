# Health & Illness - Sức Khỏe / Bệnh Nhẹ Thường Gặp

Chủ đề gắn với Lesson 15 (modal verbs). Trả lời tự nhiên bằng **should / shouldn't / must / can** khi khuyên; dùng **have got a...** để nói triệu chứng.

## 01. Do you have a healthy diet?

**Mẫu câu:**

- Yes, I think so. / Not really, but I'm trying to eat better.
- I try to eat a lot of vegetables and fruit every day.
- I drink about two litres of water. / I avoid sugary drinks.
- I have three meals a day, and I don't skip breakfast.
- I love fast food, but I only eat it once or twice a month.

Nhắc: **healthy** (khỏe mạnh, tính từ) — *a healthy diet, a healthy person*. **Health** là danh từ — *my health, in good health*.

## 02. Do you exercise? What exercise do you do?

- Yes, I exercise / work out ... times a week.
- I go jogging in the park every morning.
- I play badminton / football with my friends on the weekend.
- I do yoga at home when I don't have time to go out.
- I want to keep in shape and get some fresh air.

Nếu ít vận động, nói thật cho ngắn:

- Not really, I don't have much time. But I try to walk a lot instead of taking the elevator.

## 03. Do you often feel stressed?

- Yes, especially before an exam / when I have a lot of work.
- When I feel stressed, I usually listen to music, take a walk, or call a friend.
- I try to sleep at least seven hours every night. Sleep helps a lot.
- Sometimes I feel a bit tired, but I don't think it is serious.

## 04. What should you do if you...?

Đây là câu hỏi khuyên — dùng **should / shouldn't** + động từ nguyên mẫu.

### ... have a headache / toothache?

- You **should** take some painkillers / medicine and rest for a while.
- You **should** drink plenty of water.
- If you have a toothache, you **should** see a dentist as soon as possible.
- You **shouldn't** stare at your phone for too long.

### ... have got a cold?

- You **should** stay at home and rest. / You **should** drink hot water with lemon and honey.
- You **must** wear a mask so you don't infect other people.
- You **shouldn't** go out in the cold rain.
- If you don't feel better after a few days, you **should** see a doctor.

## 06. Cấu Trúc Dễ Nhầm

- **have got** + bệnh (BrE, thường dùng khi kể triệu chứng):
  - *I've got a headache.* / *She's got a cold.* / *He's got the flu.*
- **feel** + adjective:
  - *I feel tired / dizzy / sick.*
- **hurt / ache** (đau, động từ):
  - *My throat hurts.* / *My back aches.*
- **should / must** đứng trước V nguyên mẫu, không thêm to:
  - ✓ *You should drink water.*  ✗ *You should to drink water.*

## 07. Cách Đọc / Stress

- **head**·ache /ˈhed.eɪk/ — nhấn âm đầu, /eɪ/ đôi.
- **stom**·ach·ache /ˈstʌm.ək.eɪk/ — chú ý âm câm, "ch" đọc /k/.
- **health**·y /ˈhel.θi/ — âm /θ/ (đầu lưỡi chạm răng cửa, không thành /s/).
- ex·**er**·cise /ˈek.sə.saɪz/ — nhấn âm đầu, đuôi /aɪz/.
- **should** /ʃʊd/ — âm /ʊ/ ngắn, âm "l" câm.

## 08. Common Mistakes

- **Số ít / số nhiều "advice":** *advice* không đếm được — ~~*an advice*~~, đúng là *a piece of advice*.
- **"Sick" vs "ill":** người Mỹ dùng *sick* nhiều hơn, người Anh dùng *ill*. Trong bài thi dùng tự nhiên cái nào cũng được.
- **Đừng chia -s sau "should":** ~~*She should drinks water.*~~ → *She should drink water.*
- **Cảm cúm:** *catch a cold* (bị cảm), *have got a cold* (đang bị). Không dùng *catch the flu* ~~*catch flu*~~ — dùng **the flu** với "the".

## 09. Bài Mẫu Ngắn

I try to have a healthy diet, but I'm not perfect. I eat a lot of vegetables and fruit, and I try not to skip breakfast. I go jogging in the park two or three times a week to keep in shape. Sometimes I feel stressed before an exam, so I listen to music or take a short walk to relax. Last winter, I got a bad cold and had a sore throat. My mom told me I should drink hot water with honey and rest. I also had to wear a mask when I went out. After three days, I felt much better.
