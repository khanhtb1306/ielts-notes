# Appearance - Ngoại Hình

## 01. Câu Hỏi Thường Gặp

**What does he/she look like?**

[[audio:39]]

**What kind of hair does he/she have?**

[[audio:40]]

**What kinds of clothes does he/she often wear?**

[[audio:41]]

## 02. Age - Tuổi

- He/She is almost 25, but doesn't look that old.
- He/She is in his/her twenties/thirties/forties.
- He/She is young / old / middle-aged.

## 03. General Look - Ngoại Hình Chung

`He/She is very + adjective`: handsome, pretty, beautiful, cute, good-looking, average-looking.

## 04. Height, Weight & Build

- He/She is not very tall, about ... centimeters. / of average height.
- He/She weighs ... kilos.
- He/She is quite slim / thin / overweight / chubby / well-built.

## 05. Face & Hair

- He/She has got dark/fair skin.
- She has got long straight black hair. / He has got a beard/moustache.
- He/She has got brown/blue eyes. / glasses.

Từ vựng tóc: long, short, shoulder-length, straight, wavy, curly, black, brown, blonde.

## 06. Clothes

- He/She often wears... shirts, T-shirts, a pair of jeans, casual clothes.

## 07. Bài Mẫu Ngắn

My father is in his forties. He is not very tall, but he is well-built. He has got short black hair and brown eyes. He often wears shirts and a pair of jeans.
