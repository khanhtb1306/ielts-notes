# Family - Gia Đình

## 01. Do you live in a big family?

[[audio:37]]

**Mẫu câu:**

- No, I'm an only child. It's just me and my parents.
- No, I live in a nuclear/small family with my parents and my younger sister.
- Well, I come from quite a big family. I've got two brothers.

## 02. How many people are there in your family?

- There are four people in my family: my parents, my younger brother and me.

## 03. Do you have any brothers/sisters/siblings?

- I have one younger brother and two elder sisters.
- I've got one younger brother and two elder sisters.

## 04. What does he/she like? / What are your hobbies?

[[audio:44]]

**Mẫu câu:**

- He/She is very interested in computers.
- My sister is more interested in the arts. She likes music and loves to read.
- My dad is really into sports and swimming.
- I'm keen on movies and sports.

## 05. Cấu Trúc Nói Về Sở Thích

| Mẫu câu | Theo sau bằng | Ví dụ |
|---|---|---|
| like / love / enjoy | noun hoặc V-ing | I enjoy reading books. |
| be interested in | noun hoặc V-ing | She is interested in music. |
| be really into | noun hoặc V-ing | My dad is really into sports. |
| be keen on | noun hoặc V-ing | I'm keen on movies. |

Mở rộng lý do: *because it is interesting / relaxing / good for health / it helps me relax.*
