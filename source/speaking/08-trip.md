# Trip / Travelling - Du Lịch

## 01. Do you love travelling? Why?

- Yes, definitely, because I can relax, spend time with family and friends, learn new things and gain new experiences.
- No, I don't, because I don't have enough time / travelling is expensive.

Useful ideas: relax, spend time with family, learn new things, gain new experiences, enjoy beautiful scenery, try local food.

## 02. Tell me about one trip you remember the most (QUÁ KHỨ)

Dùng **Past Simple**. Khung: went to → went with → went by → what you did → feeling.

**Bài mẫu:**

The trip I remember the most is my trip to Hue, Da Nang, and Hoi An. I went there last year with my friends. We went there by plane, and it took us about one hour. We went sightseeing, enjoyed the local food, visited many beautiful places, and took a lot of photos. The trip was wonderful, and I had a great time. I hope to visit there again someday.

**Useful expressions:** The trip I remember the most is... / We went there by... / It took us about... / We went sightseeing. / The trip was wonderful. / I had a great time.

## 03. Describe a trip you would like to take in the future (TƯƠNG LAI)

Dùng **will / be going to**. Trả lời theo 5 câu hỏi khung:

| Câu hỏi | Ý cần nói |
|---|---|
| Where would you like to go? Why? | Place |
| Who will you go with? | People |
| How will you get there? | Means of transport |
| What will you do there? | Activities |
| How will you feel about the trip? | Feelings |

**Bài mẫu:**

I want to talk about a trip I am going to take in the future. I want to go to Da Lat because I love its cool weather and beautiful nature. I am going to go with my best friends. We are going to travel by car because it is comfortable, and it will take us about 6 hours. When we arrive, we are going to check in a nice homestay, then explore the city. We are going to visit Xuan Huong Lake and take a lot of photos. We are also going to try local foods like hot pot. I think the food will be amazing! I won't try to visit too many places in one day because I want to enjoy the peaceful feeling. I hope we will make many good memories. I am really excited about this trip.

## 05. Useful Grammar

- Present Simple, Simple Past (trip quá khứ), will / be going to (trip tương lai).
- because...; and / also; I hope to visit there again someday. (dạng đơn giản, không dùng conditional).
- Nếu muốn dùng câu điều kiện, phải chuẩn Type 1: **If** + Present, **will** + V — *If I have a chance, I will visit there again.* KHÔNG dùng ~~If I have a chance, I would love to visit again.~~ (mixed conditional sai).
