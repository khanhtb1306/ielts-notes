# House / Room - Nhà Ở Và Phòng Yêu Thích

## 01. What is your house like? How many rooms?

- Yes, my house is quite big. / No, my house is quite small.
- It has five rooms: a living room, a kitchen, a bathroom and two bedrooms.

## 02. What is your favorite room? Why?

[[audio:45]]

- My favorite room is the bedroom / living room / kitchen.

| Phòng | Lý do |
|---|---|
| Bedroom | I feel free and comfortable; I can study, listen to music, watch films. |
| Kitchen | I love cooking; I want to cook for my family. |
| Living room | I can spend time with my family; we watch TV and have a chit-chat. |

## 03. Can you describe your living room/bedroom/kitchen?

[[audio:46]]

- It is comfortable, but pretty small. / It is really big. / It is huge.
- My room is painted in blue/white/pink.
- There is a desk, a chair, a bed and a wardrobe.

## 04. Dùng Giới Từ Tả Vị Trí Đồ Vật

- My bed is in the middle of the room.
- The table is next to the bed.
- There are some pictures on the wall.
