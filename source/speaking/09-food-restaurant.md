# Food & Restaurant - Đồ Ăn Và Nhà Hàng

Chủ đề của Lesson 15, đi cùng modal verbs. Dùng lại cấu trúc thích/không thích đã học.

## 01. What are your favorite foods/drinks?

- My favorite dish is...
- I love eating/having ... for breakfast/lunch/dinner.
- My comfort dish is...
- I always eat...

## 02. Are they good for you?

- **Yes:** They are good for my health / they are healthy because they have a lot of vitamins/protein.
- **No:** They are unhealthy because they contain a lot of oil, sugar and salt.

## 03. Talk about a restaurant you went to

Dùng **Past Simple** + **modal verbs** (could / had to / should).

### Where you went

- I went to a seafood / steak / fast food restaurant with my family / friends / classmates.
- It was near my hotel / in the city center, so we `could` walk / `had to` take a taxi to get there.

### Why you went

- We went there for dinner. / It was my birthday, so we wanted to celebrate. / We wanted to try something new.

### What you ate

- To start, we had soup / salad / bread.
- The main dish was chicken rice / steak / seafood noodles.
- For dessert, we had ice cream / fruit / cake.

### The food & service

- The food was tasty / delicious / fresh. My favorite dish was... You `should` try it if you visit!
- The staff were kind, and we `could` ask them anything about the food. We `didn't have to` wait long.

### What you didn't like

- Unfortunately, it was too salty / a little oily. I had a stomachache afterwards.

### Would you go again?

- If I go back to that city, I `would` definitely eat there again! I `may` try other dishes next time.

## 04. Bài Mẫu Ngắn

Last weekend, I went to a seafood restaurant with my family. It was very popular, so we had to book a table. The main dish was grilled prawns with rice, and the spices were amazing. The staff were kind, and we could ask them anything about the food. It was a great experience — if I go back, I would definitely eat there again.
