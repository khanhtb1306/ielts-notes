# Personality - Tính Cách

## 01. What is he/she like?

[[audio:42]]

**Công thức:** `He/She is + adjective + explanation.`

- He is really neat. He always keeps things tidy.
- She is great to be with. She makes me laugh all the time.

## 02. Gợi Ý Tính Cách Và Giải Thích

| Tính cách | Giải thích mẫu |
|---|---|
| neat / organized | He always keeps things tidy. |
| great to be with | She makes me laugh all the time. |
| funny / easy-going | He always tells jokes. |
| friendly and easy to talk to | She is easy to talk to. |
| outgoing / sociable | He loves going out and meeting people. |
| kind and generous | She loves giving things and expects nothing in return. |
| shy / not very talkative | He doesn't talk much. |
| serious | It is difficult to get him to laugh. |

## 03. Is he/she friendly/talkative/humorous/generous?

[[audio:43]]

**Mức độ tính từ:**

| Mức độ | Mẫu câu |
|---|---|
| Rất | He is really/so/very + adjective. |
| Khá | He is quite/pretty/kind of + adjective. |
| Phủ định | He is not + adjective + at all. |

## 04. Ví Dụ Mẫu

My father is really neat. He always keeps things tidy. He is also great to be with because he makes me laugh all the time.
