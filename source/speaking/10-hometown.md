# Hometown - Quê Hương / Nơi Đang Sống

Chủ đề mở đầu Speaking Part 1, sau Self và trước Family. Học nhớ 2 dạng câu: (1) tên nơi/miền và (2) mô tả 1–2 nét đặc trưng.

## 01. Where do you come from?

**Mẫu câu:**

- I come from... / I'm from...
- I was born in..., but now I live in...
- I'm from a small town / a big city / a village in the north / south / centre of Vietnam.
- I've lived here for ... years. / I've lived in ... all my life.

Nếu quê khác nơi ở hiện tại thì thêm câu thứ hai để tránh bí:

- My hometown is Nam Dinh, but I live in Hanoi for school/work now.

## 02. What do you like about your hometown?

**Chọn 1–2 điểm dễ tả (đồ ăn, con người, cảnh vật, không khí, giá cả), tránh liệt kê quá 3 ý:**

- The food is really good. My favorite dish from my hometown is...
- The people are friendly and kind. Everyone knows each other.
- It is peaceful / quiet, so I can relax after a long week.
- The cost of living is low, so life is easy.
- There are beautiful lakes / rivers / mountains around the city.

## 03. Is there anything you dislike about your hometown?

- Not really, but sometimes it is too quiet / a bit boring for young people.
- There are not many good jobs. That's why I moved to Hanoi.
- The traffic is bad in the morning. / The air is polluted.
- In summer, it gets really hot. / In winter, it gets very cold.

## 04. Are there any lakes / rivers / woods / hills / mountains?

Câu ngắn khoe thiên nhiên. Học 1 danh từ + 1 tính từ mô tả là đủ.

- Yes, there is a big lake right in the centre of the city. It is called...
- We have a beautiful river running through the town.
- There are green hills / high mountains near my hometown. People often go there for a picnic on the weekend.
- No, my hometown is quite flat, but the beach is only 20 minutes away.

## 06. Cách Đọc / Stress

- **ho**·me·town — nhấn âm đầu: /ˈhoʊm.taʊn/.
- **peace**·ful — nhấn âm đầu; đọc rõ /iː/ dài.
- **cost** of **liv**·ing — nối "cost-of" thành /kɒstəv/.
- **cro·wded** — /ˈkraʊ.dɪd/, chú ý /aʊ/.

## 07. Common Mistakes

- Đừng nói *"I come to Hanoi"* — dùng **from**: *I come from Hanoi.*
- Sai giới từ nơi chốn: **in** the north/south, **on** the beach, **at** the market.
- Đừng dịch word-by-word "quê hương" thành *native land*. Dùng **hometown**.

## 08. Bài Mẫu Ngắn

I come from Nam Dinh, a small town in the north of Vietnam. It is a peaceful place, and the food is really good — my favorite dish is beef pho. The people are friendly, and everyone knows each other. However, there are not many jobs for young people, so I moved to Hanoi last year for university. I still go back home every month to visit my family.
