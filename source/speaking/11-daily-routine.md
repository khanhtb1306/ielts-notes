# Daily Routine / Time - Thời Gian Biểu Hàng Ngày

Chủ đề dùng nhiều Present Simple (thói quen) + trạng từ chỉ tần suất (always / usually / often / sometimes / rarely / never). Cần đọc trôi giờ giấc (o'clock, a.m., p.m., past, to).

## 01. What time is it in your country?

**Mẫu câu:**

- It is ... o'clock. / It is ... a.m. / p.m.
- Giờ đúng: *It is seven o'clock.* (7:00)
- Sau giờ: *It is quarter past seven.* (7:15) / *It is half past seven.* (7:30)
- Trước giờ: *It is quarter to eight.* (7:45) / *It is ten to eight.* (7:50)

Đơn giản hơn thì đọc số: *seven fifteen, seven thirty, seven forty-five*.

## 02. What time do you...

Trả lời gọn theo cặp "activity + time". Dùng Present Simple, không thêm -ing.

- get up? → *I usually get up at six thirty in the morning.*
- go to school / work? → *I go to school at seven, so I have thirty minutes to eat breakfast.*
- have breakfast / lunch / dinner? → *I have breakfast at seven, lunch at twelve, and dinner at seven p.m.*
- come home from school / work? → *I come home around five in the afternoon.*
- go to bed? → *I go to bed at about eleven at night.*

**Mẹo:** khi không nhớ giờ chính xác, thêm **around / about** trước con số cho tự nhiên.

## 03. How do you go to school / work?

- I go to school **by** bus / bike / motorbike / car / taxi.
- I walk to school because it is very near my house.
- It takes me about ... minutes / half an hour to get there.
- Ban đầu bằng: *In the morning, my dad drives me to school.* / *I take the bus.*

## 04. How often do you...?

Cấu trúc gọn: **once / twice / three times + a week / a month / a year**, hoặc trạng từ tần suất.

- ... phone your friend? → *I call my best friend once or twice a week. We chat about school and movies.*
- ... clean the house? → *I clean my room every weekend, and I help my mom clean the whole house on Sundays.*
- ... read the newspaper? → *I rarely read paper newspapers, but I read news on my phone every morning.*
- ... have friends round? → *My friends come over about twice a month. We usually watch a movie, play games and cook something simple like instant noodles.*

## 06. Trạng Từ Tần Suất

`always` (100%) > `usually` > `often` > `sometimes` > `rarely` > `never` (0%). Đặt **trước động từ chính**, **sau** to-be:

- I **always** get up at six. / I am **usually** tired on Monday.
- She **rarely** eats fast food. / He is **never** late.

## 07. Cách Đọc / Stress

- a·**bout** — nhấn âm 2: /əˈbaʊt/.
- **quar**·ter — /ˈkwɔːtə(r)/, /ɔː/ dài.
- rou·**tine** — nhấn âm cuối: /ruːˈtiːn/.
- **al**·ways — /ˈɔːl.weɪz/, chú ý /ɔː/.
- Giờ dạng "seven o'clock" đọc nối *seven-a-clock* /ˌsevn əˈklɒk/.

## 08. Common Mistakes

- **Sai giới từ giờ:** dùng **at** cho giờ cụ thể (*at 7 a.m.*), **in** cho buổi (*in the morning*), **on** cho thứ (*on Monday*).
- **Sai thì:** hoạt động thường ngày là Present Simple, KHÔNG dùng Present Continuous. *I go to school at 7* (đúng), ~~*I am going to school at 7*~~.
- **Chia số nhiều:** *two hours* (đúng), ~~*two hour*~~.
- **"Every day" viết rời** (thời gian), **"everyday" viết liền** là tính từ (thường ngày, bình thường).

## 09. Bài Mẫu Ngắn

On a normal weekday, I usually get up at six thirty. I take a quick shower, have breakfast — usually bread and milk — and leave home at around seven. I go to school by motorbike; it takes me about fifteen minutes. Classes finish at four in the afternoon, and I come home to have dinner with my family at seven. In the evening, I do my homework, watch YouTube for a bit, and go to bed at about eleven. On weekends, my routine is more relaxed: I often sleep in and hang out with my friends.
