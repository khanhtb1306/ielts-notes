# Hobbies · Weekend / Free Time - Sở Thích Và Thời Gian Rảnh

Chủ đề `hobbies` trong Speaking bank tương ứng với note này. Bao gồm hoạt động cuối tuần, thể thao, giải trí — tất cả các câu hỏi "What do you do in your free time?", "Do you like sports?", "Do you like movies?" đều nằm ở đây.

## 01. What do you often do on the weekend / in your free time?

[[audio:13]]

[[audio:14]]

**Mẫu câu:** I often + V. / I don't do anything special. / I usually go away for the weekend.

**Gợi ý hoạt động:** have a picnic, go to a disco, go out for dinner, see a movie / go to the cinema, go for coffee after class, go downtown, go shopping.

## 02. Cách Diễn Tả Thích / Không Thích

| Thích | Không thích |
|---|---|
| enjoy V-ing | can't stand something |
| be into something | hate something |
| have a passion for something | not like something |
| be keen on / be crazy about | be sick of something |
| be a big fan of | |

## 03. Do you like sports? How often?

[[audio:15]]

**Từ vựng:** work out, get some exercise, take walks, go jogging, keep in great shape, get big muscles, be really good at something.

**How often?** every day, always, usually, often, sometimes, once/twice/three times a week.

**Phủ định:** I don't like exercising at all. / I don't like anything too energetic. / I don't like to sweat.

## 04. Do you like watching movies?

[[audio:16]]

- Yes, watching movies is a good way to relax/chill out.
- No, I often spend my free time reading books / listening to music / hanging out with friends.

## 05. What kinds of movies / What is your favorite movie?

[[audio:17]]

[[audio:18]]

- My favorite movie is... It is one of the best movies I've seen.
- The story is interesting. The special effects are great. The soundtrack is excellent.
- If you want a good laugh, you should see it. It is worth seeing.

## 06. Do you like reading books / listening to music?

- I'm interested in / really into reading books about animals/nature/culture.
- I'm keen on / fond of listening to music.
- I'm a big fan of... He has a great voice. He is also handsome/attractive.
